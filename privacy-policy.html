Privacy Policy for Tabata Forge

Effective Date: May 16, 2026

Roman Repiakh ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Tabata Forge mobile application ("Tabata Forge", "the app"). This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information when you use it.

1. Information We Collect

Tabata Forge does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data with us or with third parties. The app does not use analytics, advertising SDKs, tracking SDKs, or account sign-in.

You may enter workout plans, exercise names, calories, schedule reminders, food intake, body weight, age, and gender settings inside the app. This information is stored locally on your device only and is used only to provide app features such as timers, progress tracking, calorie estimates, and workout reminders.

2. Permissions

The app requests the following permissions for the purposes described:

  • Notifications: To send workout reminders that you schedule.
  • Exact Alarms: To trigger scheduled workout reminders at the time you choose.

These permissions are used only on your device. We do not receive notification or alarm data.

3. Data Storage

All workout data, plans, food entries, settings, and reminders are stored locally on your device. We do not operate a server for Tabata Forge and do not transmit this data outside the app.

4. Data Sharing

We do not share user data with third parties.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Local app data remains on your device until you delete it in the app, clear the app data in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Because we do not collect or store your data on our servers, we cannot access, export, or delete data from your device remotely.

6. Security

Your data is stored locally using Android app storage. You should protect your device with the security options available in Android, such as screen lock and device encryption.

7. Children's Privacy

Tabata Forge is intended for general fitness use and is not directed to children. The app does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
